WHAT'S NEW IN VERSION 3.6.5:

• WEEK TIMETABLE MATRIX VIEW
Interactive 7-day (Monday to Sunday) x 6 FPT slots visual grid timetable. Toggle seamlessly between List View and Week Matrix View with a single click, with sticky headers kept in place while scrolling.

• REAL-TIME SLOT STATUS HIGHLIGHTING
Live visual status for classes: in-progress sessions feature a pulsing border, live indicator dot, and IN CLASS badge; past slots are automatically dimmed and grayscale-filtered to help you stay focused.

• ATTENDANCE TRACKER & 20% BAN GUARD
Displays detailed per-subject attendance statistics on every class card, including attended count, absent count, absence rate, and the exact remaining absences allowed before reaching FPT University's 20% exam ban threshold.

• RFC 5545 COMPLIANT QR CALENDAR EXPORT
Added unique UID and DTSTAMP properties to every exported calendar event, fixing mobile calendar batch-sync issues on Apple Calendar and Google Calendar. Added 2-week and all-upcoming export scopes.

• FAP SURVIVAL KIT (KEEP-ALIVE & 1-CLICK SURVEY)
- Auto Keep-Alive Heartbeat: Automatically refreshes your FAP session every 7 minutes while at least one FAP tab is open, preventing unexpected logouts and lost forms.
- 1-Click Lecturer Feedback: Floating Dark Glassmorphism toolbar on FAP survey pages that auto-fills 5★ ratings and inserts polite, authentic student feedback comments in one click (manual submit for student review).

WHAT'S NEW IN VERSION 3.6.0:

• OFFLINE QR PHONE SYNC
Instantly import your class and exam timetable to your phone. Scan the offline-generated QR code using your iPhone Camera or Android Google Lens to sync events directly with Apple Calendar or Google Calendar without cables or third-party apps.

• UNIFIED EXPORT MODAL & ANTI-OVERFLOW DESIGN
Consolidated file download and QR code features into a single, clean modal. Choose your preferred time range (Today, This Week, Next Week, or Upcoming Exams), download .ics files, or copy calendar data seamlessly. Optimized window dimensions eliminate bottom-edge overflow.

• TRUE DARK MODE SUPPORT
Comprehensive dark theme designed for night-time studying. Supports three modes: Automatic (follows system preference), Light, and Dark, adhering strictly to WCAG AA contrast standards.

• TODAY'S AGENDA & SLOT COUNTDOWN
A persistent hero card at the top of your schedule shows your next class slot, classroom number, instructor, and exact minute countdown. Features live status pulse indicators and same-day exam alerts.

• STUDENT GRADES TRACKER & PASS PREDICTOR
View your FAP grade breakdown, GPA, and completed credits directly in the extension. Use the interactive Target Grade slider to calculate the exact minimum Final Exam score you need to pass or reach your target grade.

• DESKTOP SCHEDULE & EXAM REMINDERS
Never miss a class with local advance notifications (15 or 30 minutes before classes; 1 hour or 1 day before exams) powered by Chrome Alarms.

• STRICT PRIVACY & ZERO EMOJIS
100% offline local processing. Student data never leaves your computer. Redesigned with clean, crisp SVG graphics and zero character emojis.

FULL CHROME WEB STORE DESCRIPTION TEMPLATE (ENGLISH & VIETNAMESE)
================================================================================

A modern Chrome extension that helps FPT University students easily view, extract, and manage their schedules from the FAP system.

Key Features:
- Synchronize weekly class timetables and exam schedules from FAP.
- 1-Click offline QR code phone sync for Apple Calendar and Google Calendar.
- Export standard .ics files compatible with iOS, macOS, Android, Windows, and Outlook.
- Today's Agenda live banner with countdown to your upcoming class slot and room info.
- Student Grade Tracker with interactive Pass Predictor and Target Grade calculator.
- Attendance risk chips: visual alerts when absences approach the exam ban limit (15% - 20%).
- Full Dark Mode support with WCAG AA compliance.
- Advance desktop notifications for upcoming classes and exams.
- 100% private and offline: student data never leaves your device.

---

Tiện ích mở rộng hiện đại dành cho sinh viên Đại học FPT giúp tra cứu, đồng bộ và quản lý thời khóa biểu từ FAP một cách dễ dàng và hiệu quả.

Tính năng nổi bật:
- Đồng bộ lịch học hàng tuần và lịch thi cuối kỳ trực tiếp từ FAP.
- Quét mã QR offline 1 chạm để thêm lịch vào Apple Calendar (iPhone) và Google Calendar.
- Xuất file chuẩn .ics tương thích với iOS, Android, macOS, Windows và Outlook.
- Bảng tóm tắt Today's Agenda đếm ngược thời gian đến slot học kế tiếp kèm thông tin phòng học.
- Tra cứu bảng điểm FAP và công cụ Dự đoán điểm thi Final Exam cần đạt để qua môn.
- Cảnh báo nguy cơ cấm thi khi tỷ lệ vắng học chạm ngưỡng 15% - 20%.
- Hỗ trợ giao diện tối (Dark Mode) chuẩn mực.
- Thông báo nhắc nhở trước giờ học và giờ thi trên máy tính.
- An toàn và bảo mật tuyệt đối: 100% xử lý tại máy khách, không lưu trữ thông tin cá nhân.
